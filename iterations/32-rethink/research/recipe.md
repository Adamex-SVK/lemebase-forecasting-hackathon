# Historical continuations at any product age

The Chronos-2 paper describes sharing information across related product histories. A diagnostic on the target shop found the eight nearest products by correlation of past weekly log-sales changes had average held-out contemporaneous correlation of only 0.030–0.059. Their previous week's change correlated between −0.020 and +0.005 with the focal product's current change. A simple correlation network has limited empirical support here. [Chronos-2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15821)

Instead, use products observed at comparable points in their selling histories. RAFT uses the continuations of retrieved similar historical segments. Here we match demand level, selling frequency, age, sale recency and recent changes and take an empirical median across their complete 13-week continuations. This is a project-specific adaptation. Iteration 16 used a product's first sale only and changed forecasts for early-life products. The current proposal can retrieve later repeat cycles, including the same product in its own past. [RAFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.04163)

Fixed before evaluation. Consider origins at four-week intervals, ending at the last week with a complete 13-week continuation inside each training panel. Start with at least 13 historical weeks, using all available points in longer summaries when less than 26 weeks exist. Drop histories with no sale in the previous 26 weeks. Compute the median positive weekly amount over 26 weeks. For 4, 8, 13, 26 weeks, use twice the selling-week share and log-one-plus mean units divided by typical positive units. Additional features are scaled log-one-plus typical positive units, recency of latest sale, capped age since first sale, and relative last positive sale size. Euclidean distance, 96 nearest donor windows, fixed row order. For each horizon, take their median units after scaling each by the ratio of query to donor typical positive sales. Give this prediction 15% of the full blend, subtracting the share from the 30% iteration 31 distribution component. Use the complete iteration 31 tree slot for series with no sales in the previous 26 weeks or an unavailable donor library. The research/results parquet files contain the two methods already combined into the new 30% slot. Reuse all other original forecast components, rounding and gates.

For pooled A–D, concatenate windows from all four pre-cut panels. Each shop's last donor week precedes its own holdout. Dates are anonymized independently. Train on the target shop's own history for its six historical origins and final export. Run peer_dependence.py and retrieval.py from the repository root with .venv/bin/python. The retrieval model is deterministic.
