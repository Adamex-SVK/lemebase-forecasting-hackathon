This criterion selected 13.4% of scored articles. The median increment was 10 units on each cut. Among eligible products, 99.46% of actual weekly quantities fell on multiples of the historical increment. The existing ensemble placed 60.09% of its forecasts between multiples. These articles accounted for 14.29% of total MASE. Product-specific repeat quantities could reflect packaging or the source's unit conventions. The records do not distinguish the cause.

For eligible predictions between increments, the location within that gap carried directional information. At an exact midpoint it was 48.1%. The probabilities are exploratory and do not themselves measure the gain from rounding each product's forecast.
