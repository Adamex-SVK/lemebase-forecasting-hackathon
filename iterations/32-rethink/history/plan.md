# Inventory-flow features

Hypothesis: the size and recency of inferred stock movements reveal forward demand beyond past sales alone. A weekly stock ledger can contain adjustments and transfers. Interpret positive flows as implied additions, rather than observed receipts.

For each series and origin, define signed flow as units plus the change between two consecutive end-of-week stocks. A transition is observed only if both stocks are available and neither was recorded negative. Use up to 13 historical transitions. Add six features before the horizon feature: share of thirteen transitions observable; fraction of available transitions with materially negative balance; fraction with materially positive balance; log1p of total positive flow divided by one plus observed-transition sales; signed log1p of net flow on the same denominator; weeks since the last positive flow within this window (13 when absent). Fractions and other summaries are missing when every transition is unobserved, including A and B. Material means greater than 1e-6 in absolute units. Denominator and mask apply per series. Fixed before any candidate outcomes.

Retain iteration 31 data, cuts, feature generation, seed, tree parameters, CDF support, threshold and lead sampling, and 30% blend slot. Report own and pooled A–D, primary 0/13/26/39 and supplementary 16/32 target offsets. The same six features enter all fits. Local execution, three tree-building threads. Save the full target model and keyed predictions. Diagnostic cohort thresholds do not route the forecasts.

The diagnostic program reads only published target weeks. Reproduce with .venv/bin/python iterations/32-rethink/history/inventory.py. Cohorts use positive current stock and complete nonnegative 14-week stock trails, then match the two extreme groups on recent number of sale weeks, volume bin and current stock cover bin. Report all six crossing summaries and sample counts in stock_matched.csv and stock_groups.csv. Each matching stratum receives the smaller group count as weight.
