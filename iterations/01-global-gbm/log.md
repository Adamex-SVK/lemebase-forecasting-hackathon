## 2026-11-21: step 1.1 feature and window builder (user said go)
- Code: `src/features.py` (ShopMatrix, `features_at`, `make_origins`, `build_windows`, `inference_features`), tests `tests/test_features.py`. Run tests: `.venv/bin/python tests/test_features.py`.
- Design as decided (D11, D20, D26, D28, D29, F6): target = units(t+h) / the series' MASE scale at the origin (scale from the zero-padded history, so it is the metric's scale); one row per (series, origin, h), h = 1..13 is a feature; windows with scale 0 are skipped; no calendar features. 41 features: lags 1-26 and rolling mean/median/max (all scaled by the scale), zero shares over 13 and 26 weeks, weeks since last sale, series age, log scale, log mean of the last 13 weeks, last `on_hand` (log, missing flag), out-of-stock flags, share of zero `on_hand` over the last 4 weeks, the shop's recent total trend (ratio of the last 4 weeks to the last 26), and `h`. Origins every 2 weeks, at least 13 weeks of history, the latest possible origin always included, all 13 target weeks inside the data.
- Tests (all pass): (1) **leakage**: features at an origin are bit-identical when units, `on_hand` and out-of-stock flags at or after that origin are replaced by random values; (2) targets equal the future units / scale, aligned with `h`, and no target week lies beyond the data; (3) scale-0 series are skipped; (4) weighted sampling keeps weights in {1, 10}; (5) **the feature `scale` equals the metric's scale** from `src/validate.py` on the real test shop at the cut (2,839 valid articles, 65 zero-scale, as in the metric); (6) inference rows are series-major with h = 1..13.

The model is mainly a learned, robust level estimator that uses how intermittent a series is; `on_hand` matters but modestly.
